REST API for strategic management of games, factions and characters. This API is in charge of defining the
campaigns and characters of a game while the tactical API is in charge of resolving the combats that occur.

== Main entities

* *Strategic Game*: A strategic game represents a campaign that can consist of several battles (tactical games). Each campaign
has a series of factions that function as if they were independent gangs.

* *Faction*: A faction is a group of characters who share resources such as items or gold.

* *Character*: Represents a character belonging to a gang with characteristics such as attributes, skills, items, etc.

== Health

Injuries, treatments, recovery and rest (workspace specs 013 and 022) under `/v1/characters/{id}`. The numbers come
from `GET /v1/healing-tables` of rmu-api-core; this repository holds only the formulas
(`domain/services/healing`).

* *Treatment difficulty*: computed on the server from `treatmentDifficulties` (procedure, injury type and severity),
one or more steps harder on the head and, when cauterizing, by the bleeding (`treatmentRules`). A procedure without a
row does not apply (400 `err-treatment-not-applicable`). The game master may still send `difficulty`, which overrides
it (stored as `difficultySource: 'override'`). `GET injuries/{injuryId}/treatment-difficulty?procedure=` previews it.
While core has no table (documents imported before spec 022) every procedure applies at medium.
* *Success*: a treatment works on a success or an absolute success, as in rmu-api-tactical; a partial success only
keeps its recovery roll modifier. Pressure stops a light bleeding and slows the rest by 4; stitches (light and medium
injuries only) stop it and halve the penalty and its recovery base, toward zero; tourniquet (limbs) and magic stop it;
cauterizing stops it (a partial success too), halves it on a failure and stores the heat critical for the game master.
An absolute failure worsens the penalty and its recovery base; the severity is computed again.
* *Healer*: a healer character subtracts its own injury penalties from Medicine and Herbalism (`healer.penalty`).
Strategic keeps no fatigue, so that is not counted.
* *Recovery*: one roll per treatment (409 `err-injury-recovery-rolled`). An untreated injury (no treatment, or a
failed one) rolls at the `none` modifier, always asks for the permanent roll and becomes permanent when it ends: with
the penalty of that roll, or all of it when it was not made (the game master can edit it).
* *Daily reduction*: front-loaded. A penalty `m` over `t` days loses `floor(m / t)` a day, and one more on each of the
first `m mod t` days.
* *Dead characters*: their injuries can be treated (the departure of the soul is out of scope), but they do not roll
recovery or rest.

== Equipment and gold

Items of characters and factions (workspace spec 023). An item is an instance of a catalog type of rmu-api-items: it
copies what it needs of it (`catalog`: cost, rarity, production hours, image; `value`), and is customized with
modifiers that keep their origin (`source.kind`: `tactical`, `manual`, `crafting`, `repair`, `catalog`). A +5 sword is
`{type: 'quality', appliesTo: 'attack', value: 5}`; a masterwork is `{type: 'quality', appliesTo: 'breakage', value: 10}`
(Strength in breakage checks).

* *Holder*: a character or a faction (its armory) of the same game, stored as `characterId` or `factionId` and
returned as `holder`. A character can act on its items (its owner); the armory and the gold of a faction are for the
game master. A player may give items of its character to its faction or to a character of that faction.
* *Gold*: `management.availableGold` of a faction, and a `gold-coin` stack of a character (coins weigh). Every write is
conditional on the version read, and an operation undoes its earlier steps when a later one fails (the Mongo of
compose is standalone, without transactions).
* *Operations* (each with an entry in `GET /v1/item-ledger`, filterable by `itemId`, `holderIds`, `gameId`, `kind`):
** `POST /v1/items/purchases`: average cost or asked `unitPrice`, times the Trading percent. A character pays with
its coins, its faction (`payer: faction`) or both (`split`); spending faction gold needs the game master.
`POST /v1/items` is kept as its alias.
** `POST /v1/items/{id}/sale`: value × sale percent × condition percent × Trading percent, unless `unitPrice`.
** `POST /v1/items/{id}/transfer`: to another holder; units of a stack join the stack of the new holder, and coins
given to a faction become its gold.
** `POST /v1/items/crafts`: Crafting maneuver on the `crafting` table of core. Materials (a percent of the average
cost, or `materialsCost`) plus `enhancementsCost` are paid and lost on a failure; a success adds the enhancements
and the Strength change of the result.
** `POST /v1/items/{id}/repairs`: maneuver with the damage of the item as a penalty (-100 if broken) on the `repair`
table of core (imported data, Core Law table 6-2); the result removes, halves or keeps the breakage modifiers, leaves
a broken item at a permanent -10, or makes it unrepairable.
** `POST /v1/items/{id}/discard` and `POST /v1/items/price-quotes` (the price of a purchase or sale without making it).
* *Trading*: an opposed percent maneuver (`GET /v1/maneuvers/percent` of core). Buying, the price is 200% less the
percent; selling, it is the percent; both between 50% and 200%. The Trading of the merchant goes in `modifiers`.
* *House rules*: `RMU_STRATEGIC_SALE_PERCENT` (50), `RMU_STRATEGIC_MATERIALS_PERCENT` (50) and
`RMU_STRATEGIC_CONDITION_PERCENTS` (`100,50,10`: intact, damaged, broken), shown by `GET /v1/economy-rules`; a request
can override the sale percent and the materials cost.

== Development

This API follows the platform and conventions of https://github.com/labcabrera/rmu-api-core[rmu-api-core] (workspace
spec 020): Node.js 24 (`nvm use`), pnpm 10 through corepack, native ESM with relative `.js` imports, SWC build, Vitest
and the hexagonal + CQRS layout of core's README (*Project layout*, *HTTP contract*).

```bash
cp .env.example .env   # host ports of the rmu-platform infrastructure (make up-infra)
pnpm install
pnpm start:dev         # http://localhost:3002, Swagger UI on /api-docs, OpenAPI JSON on /api-spec
make check             # lint, typecheck, unit and e2e tests
```

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| Command | What it does

| `pnpm build` / `pnpm typecheck` | SWC build to `dist/` / `tsc --noEmit`
| `pnpm lint` / `pnpm format` | typed ESLint + Prettier / Prettier over `src` and `test`
| `pnpm test` | unit tests (`test/unit`)
| `pnpm test:e2e` | one file per resource booting `AppModule` against mongodb-memory-server, with tokens signed against a local JWKS; calls to other APIs and the Kafka producer are replaced (`test/e2e/support`)
| `pnpm test:cov` | unit and e2e tests with the coverage thresholds of `vitest.config.ts` (what CI runs)
| `make docker-build` / `make docker-run` | image `labcabrera/rmu-api-strategic:latest` (`node:24-bookworm-slim`, `node` user, `HEALTHCHECK`); `docker-run` needs `.env.docker`
| `make create-release` | semantic-release from a clean `develop` into `main`
|===

The Joi schema in `src/config/env.schema.ts` fails startup on a missing required variable. Besides `PORT` (`3002`),
`LOG_LEVEL` (`info`) and `RMU_MONGO_STRATEGIC_URI`, the HTTP layer reads `RMU_CORS_ORIGINS` (empty: no CORS, browsers come through the
gateway), `RMU_THROTTLE_LIMIT` / `RMU_THROTTLE_TTL_MS` (`300` per `60000` ms and client IP, `0` turns it off) and
`RMU_TRUST_PROXY` (`loopback, linklocal, uniquelocal`).

Errors answer `{ statusCode, error, code, message, timestamp, path }`. Responses carry the `version` of the entity and a
`PATCH` may send it back: a stale one is a 409 `version-conflict`. `size` goes up to 100. `GET /v1/characters` and `GET /v1/items` accept up to 1000 (`CatalogPagedQueryDto`), because rmu-api-tactical loads them whole. Rule violations carry their `err-*` key in `code`, which rmu-mfe-strategic translates (the deprecated `errorCode` alias was removed in workspace spec 021).
Open problems found during the migration are listed in `docs/reports/api-template-rollout-findings.md` of the workspace.
